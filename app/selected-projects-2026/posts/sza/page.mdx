# American Express UNSTAGED—SZA

<section className="grid md:grid-cols-2 gap-8 my-8">
  <div className="bg-[rgba(255,255,255,0.05)]">
When SZA took the stage for the next installment of American Express UNSTAGED, it wasn’t just another performance—it was a celebration. Marking the anniversary of her award-winning debut album CTRL, the one-time virtual event brought fans together for an intimate digital experience that blurred the line between concert and storytelling.
\
\
Craft a visual system that matched the emotion and energy of the performance along with turning a virtual performance into an interactive fan experience.
\
\
Build a cohesive visual system across the experience, from on-screen graphics to promotional assets, that balanced the nostalgia of CTRL with the energy of UNSTAGED. The design created distinctive moments that captured attention, built anticipation, and gave fans a clear path to engage beyond the performance.
\
\
On-Screen QR Strategy, animated callouts signal exclusive merch drops, prompting fans to scan. Each moment features a custom, Amex-branded QR code that delivers seamless, instant mobile access to the drop.
\
\
**Three key QR moments**
  - Moment Coming Up: Builds anticipation ahead of the drop.
  - Scan Moment: Signals that it’s time for Card Members to scan.
  - Exclusive Member Moment:Unlocks the exclusive experience.
  - Missed Moment:Lets fans know the opportunity has passed.
