# EA Sports - Madden NFL 23

<section className="grid md:grid-cols-2 gap-8 my-8">
  <div className="bg-[rgba(255,255,255,0.05)]">
  Following the success of our Madden 22 ratings skit with Tom Brady, EA wanted to expand the concept to showcase a wider range of athletes. We created Ratings Camp—a series inspired by the unique skills and capabilities that make the game’s best athletes elite.
  \
  \
For Madden 23, EA completely reimagined its Create-a-Player experience around the iconic 99 Club rating. Bringing a major evolution to a legacy franchise required a creative approach that could make the new experience feel bigger, more entertaining, and culturally relevant. Shadowlion developed 14 pieces of content that seamlessly integrated into the storyline of the new mode, with NFL personality Chad Ochocinco helping bring the world of the 99 Club to life.
\
\
We took a human-first approach, building a visual language inspired by a football coach’s playbook. Hand-drawn illustrations, diagrams, arrows, and annotations brought a tactile, authentic feel to the campaign while making game ratings and player attributes easy to understand. Across 14 pieces, this visual system paired with athlete personality and storytelling to make the 99 Club feel personal, approachable, and culturally relevant.
