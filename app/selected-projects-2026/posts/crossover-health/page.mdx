# Crossover Health

<section className="grid md:grid-cols-2 gap-8 my-8">

Crossover Health is redefining the healthcare experience with a connected, team-based model centered on each member. By bringing primary care, mental health, health coaching, physical therapy, and specialist coordination together, Crossover creates a more seamless and personalized care journey.
\
\
When care is fragmented, people are left to navigate disconnected providers, services, and communication on their own. Crossover saw an opportunity to make healthcare feel simpler and more connected by bringing the entire care experience into one cohesive ecosystem.
\
\
To convey this innovative approach, we created a series of campaign videos that brought the member experience to life. Using warm, character-driven storytelling, motion graphics illustrating how Crossover connects each member with a dedicated care team, across specialties, and blends virtual and in-person care into one cohesive, effortless journey.
